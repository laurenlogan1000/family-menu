Heads-up

Thaw Wed night — ground chicken for Thu · Thu night — chicken thighs for Fri chili
Broth Mon night — simmer rotisserie carcass for Fri chili (~3 cups)

Sunday

Mami’s flank steak (marinade) + rice + salad

Grill or broil. Rice + salad.

Monday

Rotisserie chicken + sourdough + fruit or cut veg

Text Chris / pickup rotisserie + sourdough (or MH Butter). Fruit or cut veg. Save carcass; simmer broth tonight for Fri chili.

Tuesday

Roasted tofu + rice + broccoli (meatless)

Press tofu. Roast 425° until crisp. Rice. Steam broccoli. Ponzu / sesame / shabu.

Wednesday

Mama Huhu

Pickup. Steamed carrots.

Thursday

Chicken–Zucchini Meatballs With Feta + rice

Squeeze grated zucchini dry. Bake 425°. Go light on red-pepper flakes. Roast sliced zucchini on same sheet. Rice. Feta-lemon drizzle.

Friday

Slow-Cooker White Chicken Chili + bread + avocado

Mild green chiles; skip jalapeño and cayenne. Use Mon carcass broth (~3 cups). Slow cooker 4–6 hrs low (or stovetop ~35 min). Add beans after shredding. Bread + avocado.

Saturday

Pickup.
