Monday

Rotisserie chicken + sourdough + fruit or cut veg

Text Chris / pickup rotisserie + sourdough (or MH Butter). Fruit or cut veg. Save carcass; simmer broth tonight for Fri chili (~3 cups).

Tuesday

Mami’s pork tenderloin dry rub + rice + roasted broccoli

Sear, then roast 450° to 145°F. Rice. Roast broccoli.

Wednesday

Ginger-Dill Salmon + rice + green beans

Pat salmon dry. Ginger-dill seasoning. Roast. Rice. Steam or roast green beans. Mild for kids.

Thursday

Chicken–Zucchini Meatballs With Feta + rice + chopped tomatoes

Squeeze grated zucchini dry. Bake 425°. Go light on red-pepper flakes. Rice. Chopped tomatoes.

Friday

Slow-Cooker White Chicken Chili + bread + avocado

Mild green chiles; skip jalapeño and cayenne. Use Mon carcass broth (~3 cups). Slow cooker 4–6 hrs low (or stovetop ~35 min). Add beans after shredding. Bread + avocado.
